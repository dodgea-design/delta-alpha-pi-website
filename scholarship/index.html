Investing in student leaders

DAPi Scholarship

The annual scholarship supports high-achieving students with disabilities as they complete postsecondary education.

Fall 2027 scholarship

The current program plans to award a $2,500 scholarship for fall 2027. Application information is distributed to chapter advisors at the beginning of the spring semester.

Eligibility

  • Undergraduate in a degree or degree-leading certificate program.
  • Full-time, or approved for a reduced course load as an accommodation.
  • Active DAPi member during the 2026–2027 academic year.
  • Cumulative GPA of at least 3.33.
  • Attending college when the award is made.

Application packet

  • Cover sheet and institution information.
  • Recommendations from the chapter advisor and a faculty member.
  • Transcript showing the fall 2026 cumulative GPA.
  • Responses about leadership, advocacy, and advancing disability rights.

Deadline listed: postmarked by April 30, 2027. Questions: lgarner1@ycp.edu.
