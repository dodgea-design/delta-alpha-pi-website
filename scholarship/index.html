Investing in student leaders

DAPi Scholarship

The annual scholarship supports high-achieving students with disabilities as they complete postsecondary education.

Fall 2027 scholarship

Delta Alpha Pi plans to award a $2,500 scholarship for fall 2027. Application information is distributed to chapter advisors at the beginning of the spring semester.

Eligibility

  • Undergraduate in an associate, bachelor’s, or degree-leading certificate program.
  • Full-time, or approved for a reduced course load as an accommodation.
  • Active member of a DAPi chapter during the 2026–2027 academic year.
  • Cumulative GPA of at least 3.33.
  • Attending college or university when the award is made.

Application packet

  • Cover sheet with contact and institution information.
  • Recommendation from the chapter advisor and a faculty member.
  • Transcript showing the fall 2026 cumulative GPA.
  • Separate responses explaining how DAPi developed leadership and advocacy skills, and how the applicant will advance disability rights.
Legacy deadline shown on the current site: postmarked by April 30, 2027. Send complete applications to Lynda Garner, DAPi Scholarship Chair, York College of Pennsylvania, 441 Country Club Road, York, PA 17403. Questions: lgarner1@ycp.edu.
