Before you contact a chapter

Check with your campus

Each institution manages its own chapter activity. Contact the office that provides accessibility services or coordinates disability accommodations to ask about membership, advisors, meetings, and inductions.

Start a chapter

Bring DAPi to your campus

The current DAPi guidance begins with identifying eligible students, selecting an advisor, and completing your institution’s student-organization approval process.

Request chapter information
  1. 1
    Request information

    Email DAPi and identify a prospective chapter advisor or sponsor.

  2. 2
    Identify eligible students

    Ten eligible students are recommended, although smaller institutions have established chapters with fewer.

  3. 3
    Secure campus approval

    Review the DAPi constitution and follow your institution’s process for approving a student organization.

  4. 4
    Complete the application

    Submit the application, letter of intent, advisor information, anticipated membership, and the current one-time application fee.
