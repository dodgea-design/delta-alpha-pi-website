Resources & presentations

Materials from the legacy site are preserved here in a clearer, keyboard-friendly resource library.

AHEAD 2012 — Annotated Bibliography

Characters with Disabilities in Contemporary Fiction: Positive Portrayals, presented by Edith F. Miller and Julianne Albiero-Walton. The bibliography considers contemporary adult fiction in which a three-dimensional protagonist with a disability participates fully in the experiences, relationships, and problems of the story.

AHEAD 2013 — Webinar

Developing and Implementing a Flexible, Structured Interview to Facilitate the Accommodation Process.

AHEAD 2014 — Book Discussion

Disabilities Portrayal in Contemporary Fiction: An Ongoing Discussion. Featured works included The Cuckoo’s Calling, 1222, Unseen, and The King, with discussion questions about characterization, access, identity, and disability.
