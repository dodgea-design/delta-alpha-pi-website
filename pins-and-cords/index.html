Pins & honor cords

DAPi lapel pins and honor cords recognize member achievement. Orders are placed by chapters, not by individual students.

Order lapel pins

  1. Plan ahead. Institutional payment and purchase-order processing can take several weeks.
  2. Email the order details to dapihonor@msn.com: quantity, requester and advisor names, chapter, institution, and mailing address.
  3. Wait for the invoice. Shipping varies by order size. The legacy site lists pins at $6 each.
  4. Send payment or a purchase order to Dr. Edith F. Miller, 5540 Montauk Lane, Bethlehem, PA 18017-8909. Checks, money orders, and purchase orders are accepted; credit cards and direct deposits are not.
  5. The order ships after payment or PO is received by Priority Mail with signature confirmation.
Please confirm prices and fulfillment details by email before committing institutional funds.
