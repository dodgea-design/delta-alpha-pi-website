For chapter advisors

Pins and honor cords

DAPi lapel pins and honor cords recognize member achievement. Orders are placed by chapters, not individual students.

Order lapel pins

  1. Plan ahead for institutional payment processing.
  2. Email quantity, requester, chapter, institution, and mailing address to dapihonor@msn.com.
  3. Wait for the invoice and confirm current pricing.
  4. Send payment or a purchase order to Dr. Edith F. Miller, 5540 Montauk Lane, Bethlehem, PA 18017-8909.
  5. The order ships after payment or purchase order is received.

Order blue-and-gold honor cords

Order through your institution’s approved vendor and specify the Delta Alpha Pi combination of royal blue and gold. Confirm all prices and fulfillment details before committing institutional funds.
